Here is the complete mapping for Campus Smart Parking Simulator, with students as the vehicle owners. Every one of the 10 experiments gets at least one function, and the class names below are placeholders for now.

#ExperimentWhere it’s used in the projectFunction(s)
1Add two matricesFloor 1 and Floor 2 occupancy grids (N×N, 1 = occupied, 0 = free) are added to get the combined occupancyaddMatrices(int[][] a, int[][] b)
2Stack (max 10 integers)Keeps the last 10 parked slot numbers, so “undo last parking” pops the most recent onepush(), pop(), display()
3Student class (USN, Name, Branch, Phone)Registers N students as vehicle owners and prints their details under headingsStudent constructor, displayStudents()
4Shape, with Circle, Triangle and Square subclassesDraws a symbol on the parking map when a vehicle parks and erases it when it leaves. Circle is a bike, Square is a car and Triangle is a truck or busdraw(), erase() (polymorphism)
5Resizable interfaceA Rectangle ParkingZone implements Resizable so the zone can be expanded or shrunkresizeWidth(int), resizeHeight(int)
6Package mypackStudent, Stack and the parking-slot classes sit in mypack and are imported by the main classimport mypack.*;

8MyThread (constructor calls super, run method)Each vehicle enters as its own thread while the main thread keeps running, to show concurrent entryMyThread(String name), run()
9Enum Department with valueOf()The user types CSE, ISE, ECE or MECH and valueOf() selects that department’s parking zoneDepartment.valueOf(input)
10Wrapper classes (autoboxing and unboxing)Hours parked for three vehicles are read as Strings, converted with Integer.parseInt and then used to calculate the total and average parking feecalculateFee()

The map shows its shape (4), the occupancy grids update (1) and the slot goes on the undo stack (2). On exit, the fee is calculated from the hours (10), and the average is guarded by the exception (7). Everything is organized in the mypack package (6).
